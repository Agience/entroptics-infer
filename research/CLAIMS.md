# Claims ledger

**Rule 3 of the program, made enforceable.** Every number in `PAPER.md` or `README.md` has a row
here naming the experiment that produced it and the design it ran at. A claim with no row does not
go in the paper, and a row whose experiment no longer reproduces the number is a defect to fix.

**A row may not cite a document.** A figure quoted from prose is a figure that has drifted from the
code that made it. Every row cites a runnable script.

> **Held-constant audit.** Every experiment holds something while it sweeps, and the rows say
> which claims that leaves open. `exp01`, the central table, passes the audit (Stage 14 below),
> and `exp03` is stronger than the claim it backs (Stage 17).

**How the figures are checked.** Three gates hold the numbers to the code.
[`verify.py`](verify.py) re-runs the experiments from scratch and compares each registered figure
against what comes back; `python research/verify.py --paths` resolves all 283 registered figures
against the stored results in seconds, so a mis-registered figure is caught without a
re-measurement. [`verify_records.py`](verify_records.py) re-runs every check under `checks/` and
compares its record to four decimal places, wall clocks excepted. [`prose_check.py`](prose_check.py)
checks every figure quoted in prose against the stored results, and where a line names one
experiment its figures are checked against that experiment alone, because a stale figure usually
still appears somewhere among the pooled result files.

Run it with `python research/verify.py`, or `python research/verify.py exp03` for one.

**What the formal development assumes.** The counting argument is **conditional on the roles being
orthonormal** — true of the generator by construction, which is why the generator can say how many
relations it made, and not established for an arbitrary corpus. The theorems say what follows *given*
that structure. Nothing else in the development is owed: the null's first and second moments, the
matrix case, the horizon's termination and the counting argument are all proved.

## Stage 44 — the reader: a query carried through an ontology by its own operators

figure below as carrying a range of about that width.**
